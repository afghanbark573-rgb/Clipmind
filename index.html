Highlight note

Titles and reasons are generated from transcript wording and structural cues; the app does not yet inspect visual scenes or guarantee viral performance. Review the preview and boundaries.

Memory & processing notes

ClipMind unloads the Whisper model and FFmpeg engine after each analysis to free phone memory. Timestamp controls are built only when you open the transcript panel, and temporary audio samples are cleared after recognition. Transcript timestamps are phrase-level estimates returned by Whisper, not exact word boundaries. ClipMind will not invent highlight times if timestamp data is missing. Tap Prepare AI tools again before analyzing another range.

Fast extraction uses FFmpeg WebAssembly, which may download a large engine the first time. It is usually faster than real-time playback, but speed depends on the video format and phone. If direct extraction fails, use the real-time fallback. You can inspect only a chosen time range instead of the whole video. Very long selected ranges can still use substantial phone memory. Highlight ranking uses transcript structure and language signals, not full visual understanding. Titles and explanations are generated from transcript clues and are not proof that a moment will perform well. Keep Chrome open while processing.
